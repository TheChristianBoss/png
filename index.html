PNGTuber Face Studio v1.1

Natural photographic presets + optional feature mixer · runs offline

1 · Build your character

Add any new body pose. Map its two eye centers and mouth once; every eye and mouth option will align to that pose.

2 · Choose how to build expressions

Natural mode displays a complete original photographic sprite. No eyes, skin or mouth are pasted from different faces. Use this mode for polished PNG exports. Select Layer Mixer only when you want to test independent edits.

3 · Experimental eye and mouth mixing

Independent patch mixing is experimental. Different head angles or lighting can create seams; Natural Photo mode avoids them.

4 · Fine-tune experimental layers

Alignment matters: Each pose and expression has three face anchors. Use the controls below to nudge the selected overlay.

Tip: Select “Move eyes” or “Move mouth,” then drag directly on the preview. Arrow keys nudge by 1 pixel.

Output and automation

Natural mode exports full photographic sprites. Layer Mixer mode exports experimental combinations. All outputs are separate 1024 × 1536 transparent PNGs.

Mapping and photo quality

  • Always start with a correctly aligned transparent body pose.
  • Place three anchor points per new pose: left eye, right eye, center of mouth.
  • Import eye and mouth source photos from the same photorealistic character, not the rejected cartoon images.
  • If a patch has a visible seam, adjust its offset, scale, and tilt. For major head angles, prepare a matching-angle source.
  • Keep the entire avatar's original canvas size unchanged for smooth PNGTuber switching.
